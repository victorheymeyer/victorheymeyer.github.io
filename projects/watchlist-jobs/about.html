About Watchlist Jobs

What this tool does and how it works

About

Watchlist Jobs tracks job postings from a curated list of Tech employers plus some other Seattle based firms. More companies will be added over time. Some notable companies like Amazon, Apple, and Meta have not yet been included.

Definition of "New"

New is defined as the day after it's been posted (or first seen).

Source of jobs

We pull directly from the source (the employer's ATS) unless indicated for one-off companies.

Pages

My Jobs shows new postings filtered to your own saved criteria. My Criteria is where you set those filters. They are saved on your device or in our DB if signed in. Slug Search looks up a specific company's ATS listings. Use this tool to find ATS and Slugs for companies. Not all companies in this table are being pulled. Global shows new postings across the whole watchlist, unfiltered. Stats covers snapshot and table-level statistics for the underlying data.
