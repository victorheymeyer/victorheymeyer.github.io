About Watchlist Jobs

What this tool does and how it works

What it is

Watchlist Jobs tracks new job postings at a curated list of companies and surfaces the ones that are based in Washington State or open to remote-WA candidates. Each day's snapshot is compared against the prior one so only genuinely new roles show up as "New."

How it works

A daily pipeline pulls open roles from each company's applicant tracking system (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, and others), classifies them by discipline, role, and level, and flags whether the location looks like WA or remote-WA. The results are stored in Supabase and queried live by this site.

Pages

My Jobs shows new postings filtered to your own saved criteria. My Criteria is where you set those filters. Slug Search looks up a specific company's ATS listings. Global shows new postings across the whole watchlist, unfiltered. Stats covers snapshot and table-level statistics for the underlying data.
