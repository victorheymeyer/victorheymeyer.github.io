    Detailed Logic

    • Within a category (Area, Role, Level, Post Status, Days Old): checking multiple boxes is OR — matches any of them.
    • Across categories, plus Company, WA, Remote-WA, and Search: it's AND — a job must satisfy every filter you've set.
    • WA vs Remote-WA is the one exception: if you check either (or both), a job matches if it satisfies either one (it's an OR between those two, then ANDed with everything else).
    • Search is a plain substring match across title/company/location/department.
